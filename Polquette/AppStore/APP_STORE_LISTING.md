## Description (4000 characters max)

Polquette is a salon you can hold.

An illustrated hand waits on the screen. Choose a nail shape: almond, square, coffin, oval, stiletto, or rounded. Pick a color from a long palette. Choose a finish: gloss, matte, cream, chrome, shimmer, or glitter. Add a French tip, a gradient, or a small decoration: a sticker, a flower, a gem, a piece of foil, a pearl, or a thin line.

Tap one nail to paint that finger alone, or paint all five at once. Undo and redo are on the studio. Eight curated looks set the whole hand in one tap: Blush Pearl, Burgundy Foil, Colorful Bloom, Nude Gold Line, Midnight Chrome, Sage Cream, Coral Glitter, and Ivory French. Save a manicure into your book. It stays on this iPhone or iPad.

There is a small game beside the studio. Today’s chair asks for a specific manicure. The book of chairs is a short career of twelve appointments. Submit the hand you painted. The score is up to three stars: three when every request is met, two when only one is missed, one when something matches, and none when nothing does. One star opens the next chair. Your best score is kept.

What you will find:

- Six nail shapes and a rich color palette
- Gloss, matte, cream, chrome, shimmer, and glitter
- French tips, gradients, and decorations
- Paint one nail or all five
- Undo and redo
- Eight one-tap looks, including Blush Pearl, Burgundy Foil, Colorful Bloom, and Nude Gold Line
- A book of saved looks on the device
- A daily design chair and a twelve-chair salon career with star ratings
- Haptics you can turn off
- The privacy policy and the terms, readable in the app

Polquette is offline. It has no account, no ads, no tracking, and no purchases. It does not ask for the camera, Photos, location, or contacts.

Color on a screen is a picture of polish, not polish itself. Polquette is a game, not a cosmetic and not a salon service.

## Review notes

Polquette is a complete offline nail-design game. No sign-in, no purchase, and no network is required.

How to test:

1. Launch the app. A short introduction explains the studio. Tap Begin. The home screen shows today’s chair, the salon career, and the curated looks.
2. Tap Open the studio. The illustrated hand is the canvas. Tap a nail to paint that finger, or tap Paint all five. Try Shape, Color, Finish, French, Fade, and Decor. Undo and Redo are at the top right.
3. Open Looks and tap Use this look on Blush Pearl, Burgundy Foil, Colorful Bloom, or Nude Gold Line. The whole hand changes. Undo returns to the previous manicure.
4. Tap Save, name the look, and open Your book from the home screen. Open a saved look, or delete it.
5. Open Chairs. Today’s chair describes a manicure. Tap Design this, paint toward the note, and tap Submit this set. Stars are explained in the alert. In The book of chairs, one star on a chair opens the next. A locked chair cannot be submitted.
6. About has the privacy policy and the terms inside the app, the haptics switch, and Delete saved looks and stars. The support link is optional and needs a network only if the reviewer taps it.

iPhone is portrait. iPad is portrait, including upside down. On a wide iPad the studio places the hand and the tools side by side. There is no account and nothing to restore.
