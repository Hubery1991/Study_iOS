## Web search

A web search for the quoted string `"Polquette"` on the same day did not find an app, cosmetics brand, company, or trademark with that spelling.

The only hits were unrelated to software:

- A piano piece titled Polquette, André Offenbach, Op. 75 No. 2, listed in the July 1893 Hofmeister monthly. That is a public-domain composition title, not a product.
- The word used once as a fictional creature in an online role-playing thread.

## Names that were rejected

These were considered and not used, because an App Store listing, product, company, or trademark already used them or sat too close:

- Glossora — Glossora Beauty, a handmade press-on nail brand that claims the name.
- Nailora — an existing App Store app, Nailora: AI Nail Design Try On.
- Lacquerly — an existing App Store app, Lacquerly: Nail Color Match.
- Manivelle, Lunora, Sabelle, and Miravelle — exact `trackName` matches on the US store.
- Nacrelle — a pending USPTO word mark.
- Ivrelle — an Italian clothing brand.
- Sylvora — dance-studio software, a cosmetics filing, and a hair product.
- Glintora — a beauty brand and a USPTO filing.
- Brillora — a registered mark and beauty storefronts.
- Foilora — a wholesale aluminum-foil container brand.
- Lacquara — an active Irish company.
- Marivelle — a supplement brand and clothing shops.
- Polivern and Vernilac — existing paint and lacquering businesses.

## Conclusion

No existing App Store app named Polquette was found on the US, UK, Japan, China, Germany, France, Canada, Australia, or South Korea storefronts. The name is free to use for this original game.
