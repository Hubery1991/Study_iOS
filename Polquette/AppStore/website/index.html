A playful nail salon

Polquette

Design a manicure on an illustrated hand. Almond or coffin, blush or burgundy, gloss or foil. Save the look. Take today’s chair. Nothing leaves the device.

The looks Privacy policy

Eight places to start

One tap sets all five nails. Then change a single finger, or paint the whole hand again. The portraits are original generated images, the same ones used in the App Store screenshots.

On the hand

Six shapes

Almond, square, coffin, oval, stiletto, and rounded.

Color and finish

A full palette, then gloss, matte, cream, chrome, shimmer, or glitter.

Tips and pieces

French tips, gradients, stickers, flowers, gems, foil, pearls, and lines.

A small career

Today’s chair and twelve appointments, scored with up to three stars.

On your device only

Polquette has no account, no ads, and no network of its own. Saved looks and star ratings stay in a file on the iPhone or iPad.
