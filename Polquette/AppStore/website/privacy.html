Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

Polquette is a nail-design game. It runs on your iPhone or iPad and does not use an account.

What stays on the device

Saved looks, star ratings for the daily chair and the salon career, the haptics switch, and whether you have seen the short introduction are stored in a file on this device. They are not uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What Polquette does not do

Polquette does not collect personal information. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. It does not ask for the camera, Photos, location, contacts, or notifications. It does not connect to the network in order to play. A link in About opens this project’s website in the browser only if you tap it.

Children

Polquette is rated 4+ and is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children as a kids-category product, and it does not collect data from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the repository issue list.
