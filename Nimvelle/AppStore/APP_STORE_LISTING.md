## Description (4000 characters max)

Nimvelle is the routine you already have, written down.

Add the steps you use in the morning and the ones you use at night. Cleanser, toner, serum, moisturizer, sunscreen, mask, exfoliant, or anything else you keep. Link a step to a product on your shelf. Each day, tick off what you did. A day is complete when every active step is checked. The month shows complete days and partial days, and a streak counts the run you are on. Today can stay open: if you have not finished yet, yesterday’s streak still stands.

The shelf keeps the day you opened a jar and the period after opening printed on the pack. Nimvelle counts forward from that day and tells you when the open-jar window is close, or when it has passed. You can also note how full the jar feels, from full down to empty. That date is the maker’s storage guide. It is not a medical date, and it is not advice about your skin.

A journal is there if you want it. Write a personal note for dryness, spots, mood, and sleep. A two-week chart shows what you wrote. Nimvelle does not score your skin and does not tell you what to change. Optional progress photos sit side by side, a week or so apart. They stay in the app unless you choose to save a copy to Photos.

Reminders are off until you turn them on. A morning time and an evening time can ask iOS for a gentle local note. If that part of the routine is already finished, Nimvelle does not nudge you again the same day. There is no account and no push server.

A sample shelf is packed for the first launch, with a routine, a few products, some checked days, and a few notes, so the calendar is not empty. Remove it in About when your own products are enough. Anything you added yourself stays.

What you will find:

- Morning and evening steps you can edit, pause, or delete
- Each step linked to a product, or left unlinked
- A daily check-off, including earlier days
- A month calendar with complete and partial days
- A current streak and a longest streak
- A shelf with open date, period after opening, and how much is left
- A quiet warning before an open-jar window ends
- Optional notes for dryness, spots, mood, and sleep
- A simple trend for the last two weeks
- Optional progress photos, compared side by side, stored on the device
- Optional local reminders you can turn off
- A sample shelf you can remove
- Haptics you can turn off
- The privacy policy and the terms, readable in the app

Nimvelle has no account, no ads, no tracking, and no purchases. If you deny the camera, you can still choose a photo or skip photos. If you deny Photos, you can still use the camera. If you deny notifications, the check-in still works. Nothing you log is uploaded.

Nimvelle is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor a condition. A streak is a count of steps you listed. A note is a memory. Decisions about your skin belong to you.

## App Privacy

Data Not Collected.

- No data is used for tracking
- Tracking: No

- Privacy Nutrition Label: Data Not Collected
- The privacy manifest (`App/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`) declares no tracking and no collected data types

The camera and the photo library are used only on device for optional progress photos. Pictures are stored in the app container. A photo is written to the Photos library only when the user taps Save a copy to Photos. Nothing is uploaded to a developer server.

Notifications are local. If the user turns a reminder on, iOS schedules it on the device. ## Review notes

Nimvelle is a complete offline routine journal. No sign-in, no purchase, and no network is required to check in.

The camera and Photos are used only for optional progress photos. Those pictures stay in the app container. They are not uploaded. Save a copy to Photos writes a copy only when the reviewer taps that button.

Notifications are optional and off by default. The sample shelf does not schedule a reminder. Turning a reminder on is what triggers the system permission. There is no remote notification.

Nimvelle is not a medical device. Copy in the shelf, the journal, About, and the terms says so. It does not diagnose skin or recommend treatment. The open-jar window is the period after opening printed on a cosmetic pack.

How to test:

1. Launch the app. A sample shelf is already there, with checked days and a few notes. Today opens on Morning before evening, or Evening from 5 pm. Three of the five morning steps are already checked. The streak is 5 days, because today is not finished and the five days before it are.
2. Tick Moisturize and Sunscreen, or tap Mark the rest done. Morning shows 5 of 5. Open Month. Today becomes complete once Evening is finished too. The streak includes today only when every active step is checked.
3. Month shows November with complete days, a partial day, and today. Move to another month and back. Tap an earlier day and toggle a step. That day updates. A future day can be viewed and explains that check-ins start when the day arrives.
4. Shelf lists Night Repair Cream first, past its open-jar window, then Veil Cream, close to the end of its window. Open a product. Change how much is left, or the period after opening, and save. The status line updates. Add a product of your own.
5. Journal, Notes: the two-week chart uses the sample notes. Save a note for today. The chart and the recent list include it. The screen says the notes are not an assessment.
6. Journal, Progress: tap Add a photo, then Take a photo. Allow the camera. The picture appears on the device and can be compared once a second photo exists. Deny the camera instead: the alert explains Settings, and Choose a photo still works. Deny Photos: the alert explains that, and the camera path still works. On a saved photo, tap Save a copy to Photos. Allow adding. The picture is saved by the system. Nimvelle does not upload it. If adding is denied, the journal says so and the copy in the app remains.
7. About: turn on Morning reminder. Allow notifications. A local reminder is scheduled. Deny notifications: About explains Settings, the toggle stays off, and the routine still works. Turn haptics off if you want silent taps.
8. About, Remove the sample. Confirm. The sample products, steps, check-ins, and notes are removed. A product or step you added in steps 4 or earlier remains.
9. About has the privacy policy and the terms inside the app. The support link is optional and needs a network only if the reviewer taps it.

iPhone is portrait. iPad is portrait, including upside down. There is no account and nothing to restore.
