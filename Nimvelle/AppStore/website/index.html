SKINCARE CHECK-IN

The morning, already laid out.

Build the routine you actually use. Check off the morning and the evening. Keep a streak, and notice when a jar is near the end of its open-jar window. Nothing is uploaded.

Nimvelle is a cosmetic journal for your own shelf. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose your skin.

What you keep

Today

A morning list and an evening list. Each step can point at a product on your shelf. Tick what you did. A finished day extends the streak. Today can stay open until you are done.

Month

A calendar of complete and partial days, with the streak and the longest run beside it. Open any day and check it in, including yesterday.

Shelf

Open date, the period after opening printed on the pack, and a note for how full the jar feels. Nimvelle warns before that window ends. The date is a maker’s storage guide, not a medical date.

Journal

Optional notes for dryness, spots, mood, and sleep, with a simple two-week chart. Optional progress photos, compared side by side, stored only on the device. Reminders are local and off until you ask.

A sample shelf is included so the first launch is not empty. Remove it in About when your own products are enough. Notes and photos are memories, not an assessment.
