## Description (4000 characters max)

Lookmira is a diary for makeup you already finished.

Write down the look: the day, a title, one or more styles, the products you used, a note, and a photo from your library. Natural, work, evening, weekend, and party are built in, and you can pick more than one. A product is a name you type, such as a lipstick, an eyeshadow, a foundation, a blush, a liner, or a brow pencil. It is a line in that look, not a separate wardrobe of shades.

The calendar shows which days have a look. Open a day to see the photo and the list. Mark a favorite when you want it back. Recreate this look copies the title, styles, products, notes, and photo onto another day, so you can wear it again without retyping it. Search finds a look by a style or by a product name.

The first time you open Lookmira, three example looks are already there, so the diary is not empty. They are ordinary entries. Delete them when your own looks are enough.

A reminder to log today’s look is off until you turn it on. If you do, it is a local notification on this iPhone, at the time you choose. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

What you will find:

- A look with a date, a title, a photo, styles, products, and notes
- Create, edit, and delete
- A calendar of the days you logged
- Favorites
- Recreate this look, and a recents list
- Search by style or product name
- Example looks on first launch, which you can delete
- An optional reminder that stays off until you opt in
- The privacy policy and the terms, readable in the app

Lookmira has no account, no ads, no tracking, and no purchases. The diary does not need a network. If you deny Photos, you can still write a look without a picture.

This is a journal of looks you finished. It does not try makeup on a live camera. The products you list are notes beside a photo, not an inventory and not a shop.
