Lookmira
Privacy Terms

A diary of finished looks.

Lookmira is an offline journal for makeup you already wore. Save a photo, the products you used, a style, and a note. Find the look again on the calendar, in favorites, or by searching a tag or a product name.

On your iPhone

  • Log the date, a title, style tags, products, notes, and a photo from your library.
  • Browse the month and open the day.
  • Keep a favorite and recreate that look on another day.
  • Turn on a local reminder only if you want one. It stays off until you do.

There is no account. The diary does not need a network. Photos you add stay in the app on the device.

Lookmira is a notebook of looks you finished. It does not try makeup on a live camera, and the products in a look are notes, not a shop or an inventory.
