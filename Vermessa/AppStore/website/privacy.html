Vermessa

Privacy

The lipstick drawer stays on your device.

Home Terms

What is stored

If you use Vermessa, the app can store lipstick names, optional brands, finishes, undertones, color families, occasion tags, shade colors, wishlist items, the dates you log a lip color, and optional photos you choose. That information is written in the app’s private storage on the iPhone or iPad.

What is not collected

Vermessa has no account. It does not ask you to sign in. It does not include advertising or analytics. It does not track you across apps or websites. The wardrobe works without a network connection.

Photos

A picture of a tube or a swatch is optional. You pick it from your photo library. Vermessa does not open the camera, and the picture is not uploaded. Still studio cards bundled with the app are part of the product, not photos of you.

Deleting

Delete a lipstick in the app to remove that shade, its photo, and its wear notes. Delete Vermessa to remove the whole drawer from the device.

Children

Vermessa is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on the App Store listing for Vermessa.
