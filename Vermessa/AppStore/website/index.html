Vermessa

A drawer for the lipsticks you own.

Catalog shades, filter them, and get picks from your own tubes. Offline, on your iPhone or iPad. Not a live mirror.

Privacy Terms

What it keeps

  • Name, optional brand, finish, undertone, color family, and occasion tags
  • A shade chip, plus an optional photo of the tube or a swatch
  • A wishlist, separate from the colors you can wear today
  • A short log of which lip color you wore

What it does not do

Vermessa does not open the camera, track a face, or paint lipstick onto a live picture. There is no account and no network requirement for the wardrobe.
