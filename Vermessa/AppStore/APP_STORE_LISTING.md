## Description

Vermessa is a lipstick wardrobe for the colors you already own.

Add each tube with a name, an optional brand, a finish, an undertone, a color family, occasion tags, and a shade chip. A photo of the tube or a swatch is optional and stays on your iPhone or iPad.

Browse the drawer as a grid. Narrow it by finish, undertone, color family, or occasion, or search by name and brand. A separate wishlist holds shades you have not bought yet, so they never show up as something you can wear today.

Picks come only from the lipsticks in your drawer. Choose an occasion and an undertone, and Vermessa ranks your own shades by how well they fit and how long it has been since you wore them. Shades that have been quiet rise above the one you wore this morning.

Worn is a short log of lip color: what you put on today, and the days before. It is not a makeup diary, a skincare calendar, or a closet.

Vermessa does not open the camera, does not track a face, and does not paint color onto a live picture. A few families include a still studio card so you can see the mood of a shade. Everything else is a color chip you set yourself.

There is no account. The drawer is stored on the device. The core of the app does not need a network connection.
