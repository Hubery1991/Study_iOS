Clavewell

Drop motes. Match three. Keep the rim clear. An original puzzle for iPhone and iPad, played entirely on your device.

How it plays Privacy policy

How a well is played

There is no account, no network, and nothing to buy. Tap a column. The mote falls. Three or more of the same color that touch along an edge ring and clear, and anything above them drops into the gap.

  1. Resonate. Corners do not count. Only edges do.
  2. Chain. If a fall makes another group, it rings too, and the points multiply.
  3. Spark. Every 12th mote bursts the colors touching it.
  4. Keep the rim clear. In Cadence, a new row climbs from the deep. If that rise would pass the rim, the well ends.

Three wells

Cadence

A row rises from the deep, and the pace quickens the longer you last.

Stillwater

Nothing rises. Clear space until no column can take another mote.

Flare

Seventy-five seconds. Every clear gives a little time back.

On your device only

High scores, settings, and the how-to-play flag stay in local storage. Clavewell does not collect personal data, show ads, or include purchases. Sound can be turned off. Color patterns and a landing guide are there if you want them.

The art is drawn in the app. The tones are synthesized when you play. Nothing is downloaded.
