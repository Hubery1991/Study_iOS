Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy describes Clavewell, a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad by Jacky Liu ("we"). It explains what the game does with information. The short version is that Clavewell does not collect personal data.

The short version

Clavewell does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not require an account or a network connection. You can play the entire game with the device offline.

What stays on your device

The game stores a few things locally, using iOS preferences on the device: your high scores and lifetime play totals; the settings you choose, including sound, haptics, color patterns, and the landing guide; and whether you have already opened the how-to-play guide. Deleting Clavewell deletes this information. We have no server that receives it.

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, advertising identifier, or payment information. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no third-party software kits. The game does not ask you to sign in, and it does not contact a network service of ours.

Apple

If you download Clavewell from the App Store, Apple handles that purchase and download under Apple's own privacy policy. That relationship is between you and Apple. Clavewell does not receive your App Store account details.

Children

Clavewell is a general-audience puzzle. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will show a new effective date on this page and in the app. The in-app copy updates when you install an update of the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to Jacky Liu via https://github.com/Hubery1991/Study_iOS/issues.
