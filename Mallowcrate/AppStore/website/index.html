Mallowcrate

Nudge every honey crate onto a daisy.

Mallowcrate is a cozy crate-pushing puzzle for iPhone and iPad. You walk Mallow, a round marshmallow courier, one tile at a time and nudge a single crate onto each daisy. Thirty-six handcrafted levels grow from a first nudge to a tricky meadow.

Privacy policy Terms of use
Real pushing rules Walk or swipe. Push one crate. You cannot pull, and two crates will not move together.
Stars and best moves Par is the shortest solve. Stay close for three stars. Your best stays on the device.
Undo and restart A sideways nudge is never the end of the try. Step back or start the meadow over.
Quiet by design No account, no ads, no purchases, and no network is required to play.
