## Description (4000 characters max)

Mallowcrate is a cozy puzzle about pushing honey crates onto daisies.

Mallow is a round marshmallow courier with a sprout on their head. The meadow is full of chunky crates, and every daisy pad is waiting for one. You walk one tile at a time. Walk into a crate and it nudges forward. You cannot pull a crate, and you cannot shove two at once. The level is done when every daisy is covered.

Thirty-six handcrafted levels are split into six meadows: Meadow, Orchard, Clover, Honey, Lantern, and Starlight. The first nudge is a single step. Later shelves ask you to walk the long way around, park a crate, and mind the order. Each level has a par, the fewest steps it takes. Finish within two steps of par for three stars. A wider band earns two. Any clear earns one. Your best move count is kept on this device.

Play with a swipe, the on-screen pad, or the arrow keys on a keyboard. Hold a direction to keep walking. Undo steps back. Restart puts the crates where they began. A crate that lands on a daisy bounces and sparkles, and a finished level gets a small celebration.

What you will find:

- Classic one-step pushing rules
- Swipe controls and a chunky direction pad
- Keyboard arrows, plus U to undo and R to restart
- Undo, restart, and a live move counter
- A level select with locks, stars, and best scores
- Six worlds and thirty-six original levels
- Sound and haptics you can turn off
- A how-to-play guide and an About screen
- The privacy policy and terms, readable in the app

Mallowcrate is offline. It has no account, no ads, no tracking, and no purchases. Progress, stars, and the sound and haptics switches stay in local storage on your iPhone or iPad. Delete the app, or tap Reset Progress, and that local save is gone.

Made for a quiet minute and a slightly harder minute after that.

## Review notes

Mallowcrate is a complete offline puzzle. No sign-in, no purchase, and no network is required.

How to test:

1. Launch the app. Tap Play. Level 1, First Nudge, is solved by one push to the right (swipe right, or the right pad button).
2. The clear screen shows stars and moves. Tap Next level.
3. Open Levels from the home screen. Level 2 is unlocked. Later levels stay locked until the previous one is cleared.
4. During a level, Undo steps back and Restart restores the crates. The move counter includes walks and pushes.
5. Settings has Sound and Haptics. Reset Progress clears stars and best moves and asks first.
6. About opens the privacy policy and the terms inside the app. The support link is optional and needs a network only if the reviewer taps it.

iPhone is portrait. iPad is portrait, including upside down. There is no account and nothing to restore.
